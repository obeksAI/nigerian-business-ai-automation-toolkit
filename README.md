## What is Business AI Automation?

Business AI automation involves using artificial intelligence to handle repetitive business operations, customer interactions, administrative responsibilities and sales processes.

Instead of relying entirely on manual work, businesses can deploy AI agents to perform specific tasks.

## WhatsApp AI Automation in Nigeria

WhatsApp is an important communication channel for Nigerian businesses.

AI agents can help businesses:
- Answer customer questions.
- Recommend products.
- Collect orders.
- Provide business information.
- Handle customer enquiries.
- Escalate complicated requests to employees.

## What Should You Look for in an AI Automation Platform?

1. No-code agent creation.
2. WhatsApp integration.
